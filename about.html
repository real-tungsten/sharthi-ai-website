About Tungsten AI

An initiative to make artificial intelligence offline, accessible, and completely safe for students across India.

Our Mission

Mainstream AI requires high-speed internet and expensive hardware. Tungsten AI breaks this barrier by running locally on standard computer systems, empowering classrooms even in remote regions.
